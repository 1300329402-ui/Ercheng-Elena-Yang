About me

I am a student theatre artist at BASIS International School Nanjing, with experience in musical theatre, film, and devised performance. My work spans acting, directing, scriptwriting, editing, and visual design.

From performing onstage to developing original works and directing a contemporary film adaptation, I enjoy exploring different ways to tell stories. I am especially interested in the complexity of human behavior and how theatre brings different perspectives and experiences to life.

I've played heroes and villains, brought stories to life, and learned to embrace the unexpected.
And somehow, I always hear:
“You certainly make it work.”
